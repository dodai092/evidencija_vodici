# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Single owner/director at FreeSpirit travel agency. Expert user reviewing the dashboard regularly — weekly or more. Uses it solo to monitor guide production statistics, tour performance, and financial P&L across cities and years. No hand-holding needed; the design can reward familiarity and density.

## Product Purpose

Internal business intelligence dashboard tracking guide production for FreeSpirit travel agency. Four tabs: full-year 2025, YTD 2026, 2025-vs-2026 comparison, and a Management financial dashboard (P&L, guide performance, channels, operational insights, city breakdown). Success means arriving at the right number instantly and trusting the data completely.

## Brand Personality

Precise · professional · calm. Bloomberg terminal-adjacent — authoritative, data-first, no noise. The serif display type (Playfair Display) is intentional editorial restraint, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS startup dashboards**: purple-pink gradients, floating cards, animated counters, Mixpanel/Amplitude aesthetic — feels cheap next to the data weight this tool carries.
- **Consumer app playfulness**: rounded-everything, bright palette, Notion/Linear consumer aesthetic — wrong register entirely for a solo director reviewing P&L.

## Design Principles

1. **Data before decoration** — every visual element earns its place by helping the user find a number faster or trust it more.
2. **Hierarchy through typography, not color** — Playfair Display at display scale, Montserrat for UI, Inconsolata for data. Color is signal (delta, city, year) not decoration.
3. **Expert user assumed** — density is a feature, not a flaw. Don't pad or explain what the director already knows.
4. **Consistent signal vocabulary** — green/red for deltas, teal for primary accent, year colors (indigo 2025, green 2026) used consistently everywhere.
5. **Calm under data pressure** — even complex multi-year comparisons should feel settled, not frantic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. 4.5:1 contrast for body text, keyboard navigation, visible focus states. Dark mode is a first-class supported variant (not an afterthought).
